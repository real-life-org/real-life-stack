# Read me first

This Storybook shows Real Life Stack **running**: every page has a story with real building blocks on top of a real data source. What you click takes effect, until you reload. It answers three questions:

- **How is an app composed?** → section **App**, read in order.
- **What does a building block look like, and what does it do without this or that capability?** → **App shell**, **Modules**, **Items**.
- **Which hooks exist, and what do they answer?** → **Foundations**.

The handbook, with its paths for vibecoders, agents, contributors and operators: [real-life-stack.de/handbuch](https://real-life-stack.de/handbuch/). The normative truth is the [specification](https://github.com/real-life-org/real-life-stack/blob/master/docs/spec/README.md) (German).

The user interface inside the stories is German today: the toolkit's own texts are not translated yet. The explanations here are English; the code they explain is the same either way.

## The path through the app

One example runs through everything: a community garden organises a harvest festival. It has a date and a place, so **the same item** appears in the calendar and on the map.

1. [00 Community garden](?path=/docs/rls-handbook-app--docs) — the app as a whole: switch space, switch module, open and edit an item.
2. [01 The app in 40 lines](?path=/docs/rls-app-01-the-app-in-40-lines--docs) — what an app writes, and what the frame takes off its hands.
3. [02 The register](?path=/docs/rls-app-register--docs) — the one list of modules; extend and replace.
4. [03 The module host](?path=/docs/rls-module-host--docs) — how an entry becomes a surface.
5. [04 The loading contract](?path=/docs/rls-app-ladevertrag--docs) — what a module loads, and why.
6. [05 Focus, panel, create](?path=/docs/rls-app-fokus--docs) — where it is recorded which item is open.
7. [06 A module of your own](?path=/docs/rls-app-06-a-module-of-your-own--docs) — three steps, shown on the marketplace.

## After that, by question

- **App shell** — header, space switcher, navigation, the shared panel, sign-in, contacts. The scaffold around the module surface.
- **Spaces** — the space as working context: create, configure, switch, membership.
- **Modules** — the seven surfaces of the toolkit, feed, kanban, calendar, map, resonance, list, graph: one overview each inside the host, below it the views and the shared tools (search, filter, create).
- **Items** — what is the same in every module: the preview card, the detail view, comments and reactions, the create form.
- **Foundations** — all hooks with the question they answer and what happens without the capability; the UI primitives; the [terms](?path=/docs/rls-foundations-terms--docs).
