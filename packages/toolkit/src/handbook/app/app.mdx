# The app in 40 lines

An app on Real Life Stack provides **four things**: a connector to its data, a router, its register, and the frame from the toolkit. Everything else — header, space switcher, tabs, the shared panel, detail, create, activity, search and filter, the guard against losing a draft — comes from `RoutedAppFrame` (spec 01, "what stays with the app").
