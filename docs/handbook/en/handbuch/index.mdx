**Real Life Stack is a modular toolkit for community apps.** An app consists of a connector to its data, a register of its modules and the frame from the toolkit; map, calendar, kanban, feed, list, graph and resonance come ready-made. This handbook leads there on four ways. You only need the one you are up to.

## Where the truth lives

The [specification](https://github.com/real-life-org/real-life-stack/blob/master/docs/spec/README.md) is normative (in German); in case of conflict it wins. This handbook explains and points, it decides nothing. For agents the same pages exist as Markdown under [/llms.txt](/llms.txt). The German edition leads; English follows page by page.
